# Payment options for your Lagos shop: Paystack, Flutterwave and Moniepoint

## Short summary

I looked at three well-known Nigerian fintechs that can help a small shop take payments: Paystack, Flutterwave and Moniepoint. All are real, established companies with African roots, but they were built for slightly different purposes. The sources do not list any prices or fees, so you will need to ask each provider directly before deciding.

## The options found

### Paystack
- **What it does:** A Nigerian payment processor that helps businesses in Africa take payments. It was started in 2015 by Shola Akinlade and Ezra Olubi, joined Y Combinator in 2016, and was bought by Stripe in October 2020 for over $200 million. At the time of the sale, it served about 60,000 merchants.
- **Price:** price not listed.

### Flutterwave
- **What it does:** Payment infrastructure for global merchants and payment service providers. It was founded in 2016 by Iyinoluwa Aboyeji and Olugbenga Agboola. It is headquartered in San Francisco and operates across Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda, Ghana, South Africa and many other African countries. It raised $170 million in 2021 at over $1 billion valuation, and $250 million in 2022 at over $3 billion valuation.
- **Price:** price not listed.

### Moniepoint (formerly TeamApt)
- **What it does:** A Lagos-based fintech that offers banking services and loans through an online banking app. Founded in 2015 by Tosin Eniolorunda and Felix Ike. It received a Central Bank of Nigeria switching license in November 2019, raised $5.5 million in Series A funding in March 2019, and in January 2026 its microfinance bank license was upgraded to national status by the CBN.
- **Price:** price not listed.

## What it means for your shop

If you want a service that is focused directly on African merchants and is backed by Stripe, Paystack fits that profile. If you want broader reach beyond Nigeria and a company with very large international funding, Flutterwave matches that description. If you want a Lagos-based company with a CBN switching license, banking services, loans and a recently upgraded national microfinance bank license, Moniepoint stands out.

Because the sources do not say what each one charges per transaction, how fast money settles into your account, what a POS terminal costs, or whether each supports cards, bank transfers, USSD and cash/agent payments, treat this as a starting point, not a final decision.
