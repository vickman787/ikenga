## The report

## Short summary

I looked at three payment options often used by Nigerian small businesses: Paystack, Flutterwave, and Moniepoint. None of the sources I checked listed exact prices, so you’ll need to ask each provider directly before deciding. For a Lagos shop, the right choice mostly depends on whether you sell face-to-face, online, or both.

## The options

### Paystack
Paystack is a Nigerian fintech founded in 2015 by Shola Akinlade and Ezra Olubi. It helps businesses take payments and was acquired by Stripe in October 2020 for over $200 million. At the time of the acquisition, it served about 60,000 merchants.

**Price:** price not listed

### Flutterwave
Flutterwave was founded in 2016 and provides payment infrastructure for merchants and payment providers. It is headquartered in San Francisco with operations in Nigeria and other African countries. It reached a valuation of over $1 billion in 2021 and over $3 billion in 2022. It also offers a remittance service called Send App for sending money into Africa.

**Price:** price not listed

### Moniepoint
Moniepoint is based in Lagos and was founded in 2015 as TeamApt by Tosin Eniolorunda and Felix Ike. It provides banking services and loans through an online banking app. It received a Central Bank of Nigeria switching license in November 2019, launched personal banking in July 2023, and its microfinance bank subsidiary was upgraded to a national license by January 2026.

**Price:** price not listed

## What it means for your Lagos shop

If most of your customers walk into your shop and pay with cards or POS, think about in-person suitability, how quickly the money reaches your account, and whether the provider is properly licensed by the CBN. Moniepoint’s CBN switching license and banking/microfinance background may be useful if you want payment and banking services in one place.

If you sell online, Paystack has a strong local merchant focus in Nigeria, Flutterwave offers wider reach across multiple countries, and Moniepoint brings banking and loan services alongside payments. If you need a website or app connection, ask each provider about setup help and ongoing support.

## Things to watch out for

- **No prices were given in the sources.** Do not guess fees; contact Paystack, Flutterwave, and Moniepoint directly for current transaction costs, setup charges, and settlement timing.
- **Sources do not clearly compare in-person/POS performance** for each provider, so confirm which works best with your terminal or card reader.
- **Comparison articles exist**, but the sources I reviewed did not give reliable, fee-level details, so treat blog comparisons as a starting point only.
- Some facts are company background rather than shop-specific advice, such as valuations and headquarters location, so weigh them lightly against your daily costs and customer needs.
